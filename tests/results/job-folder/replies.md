# My recruiter replies

Three short replies, in my voice. Claude fills only the slots in angle brackets and shows me the full text before anything is sent.
Edit the wording to sound like you. Keep each under about 90 words.

The one rule: my calendar link goes out only in reply B, and only after the role passes every fit check.

---

## A. Questions first (fit check has Unknowns and no Fails)

No calendar link. No pay number. Ask only what is unknown, at most 3 questions.

```
Hi <their first name>,

Thanks for reaching out. I'm open to <kind of work from my profile> right now, and this could be a fit.

<A few questions | One question> before we set up a call:
1. What is the pay range, or the hourly budget if it's a contract?
2. Is it remote? I'm near Riverton, so hybrid within 25 miles also works.
3. What are the interview steps?

Thanks,
Riley
```

Swap-in questions (use when that unknown matters more):
- Agency that did not name the company: `Which company is it, and are you working with their hiring team directly?`
- Level unclear: `What level is the role, and how many years of experience are you looking for?`
- Unclear if contract: `Is it full-time or contract? If contract, how long and how many hours a week?`
- Take-home rules matter to me: `If there's a take-home, how long is it, and can I use AI tools on it?`

Drop a question the email already answers, and renumber. If only one is left, write "One question".

---

## B. Let's talk (every fit check passes)

```
Hi <their first name>,

Thanks for the details. This sounds like a real match: <one reason, using only their facts>.

I'd be glad to talk. What times work for you this week?

My resume is attached. Please don't submit my profile anywhere until we've talked.

Thanks,
Riley
```

- I have no calendar link yet, so the line stays "What times work for you this week?". If I add a link to profile.md, Claude offers "You can pick a time here: <link>" instead.
- The reason uses only facts from their message or the post.
- In-house recruiter (works at the company itself): delete "Please don't submit my profile anywhere until we've talked."

---

## C. No thanks (any fit check fails)

```
Hi <their first name>,

Thanks for thinking of me. This one isn't a fit for me: <reason>.

If you have operations coordination or project coordination roles later, I'd be glad to hear about them.

Thanks,
Riley
```

Reason lines (pick the first failing check; never state a number):
- Pay below my floor: `the range is below where I need to be`
- Onsite or relocation: `I'm looking for remote, or hybrid within 25 miles of Riverton`
- Level too senior: `it asks for more experience than I have`
- Level too junior: `it's set up for an earlier career stage than mine`
- Process: `the interview process doesn't fit what I can do right now`
- Far from my background: `it's further from my background than would be useful to you`

---

## How Claude picks a reply

1. Not a real person about a real role (job alert, newsletter, bulk blast, task platform, likely scam): no reply. Log it.
2. I already replied on the thread: do not start over with A. Read my last message and their answer.
3. Score the fit check. Any Fail: C. No Fail but Unknowns: A. Everything passes: B.
4. Exception: a full-time role whose pay top is below my floor, with good benefits or other upside, is my call. Claude asks me before writing C.
5. After they answer A, score again. They dodged the pay question: do not ask twice; tell me.
6. They ask my number first: for salaried roles, say I'd rather hear the range for the level first. For a contract, Claude may offer my ask from my profile, never below my floor, and only after I approve the exact text.
